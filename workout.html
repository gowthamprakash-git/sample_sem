Prescribed Exercises by Targeted Muscle

Targeted Muscle: Upper Chest (Clavicular Head)

1. Incline Dumbbell Bench Press

⚙ Dumbbells ★ Primary Compound

Biomechanical Cue: Set bench incline to 30 degrees. Retract and depress scapulae. Lower dumbbells until thumbs align with upper armpits, then press upward along clavicular pennation lines.

Target Head Upper Clavicular
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 8-10
Rest 90 - 120s
Intensity RPE 8.5
Targeted Muscle: Middle Chest (Sternal Head)

2. Flat Barbell Bench Press

⚙ Barbell ★ Power & Mass

Biomechanical Cue: Grip slightly wider than shoulder width. Maintain thoracic arch with glutes planted. Lower bar to mid-sternum, drive through floor, and press explosively.

Target Head Sternal Mid-Pec
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 6-8
Rest 120 - 180s
Intensity RPE 9.0
Targeted Muscle: Lower Chest (Abdominal Head)

3. High-to-Low Cable Crossover Flyes

⚙ Cable Tower ★ Peak Squeeze

Biomechanical Cue: Position pulleys above shoulder height. Stagger stance with slight forward lean. Sweep hands downward across lower torso, squeezing lower chest for 1 full second.

Target Head Abdominal Head
Sets & Reps 3 Sets × 12-15
Rest 60 - 75s
Intensity RPE 8.0
Targeted Muscle: Quadriceps (4 Heads)

4. Barbell Back Squat & Leg Extensions

⚙ Barbell / Machine ★ Lower Foundation

Biomechanical Cue: Bar on upper traps. Inhale and brace core 360 degrees. Break at hips and knees together, descend to parallel depth, and drive upward through mid-foot.

Target Head All 4 Quad Heads
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 6-8
Rest 120 - 180s
Intensity RPE 8.5
Targeted Muscle: Hamstrings (Biceps Femoris)

5. Romanian Deadlift (RDL)

⚙ Barbell / Dumbbells ★ Posterior Chain

Biomechanical Cue: Soft knees. Push hips backward toward wall behind you while keeping bar skimming against shins. Feel deep hamstring stretch before driving hips forward.

Target Head Biceps Femoris
Sets & Reps 3 Sets × 8-10
Rest 90 - 120s
Intensity RPE 8.0
Targeted Muscle: Calves (Gastrocnemius & Soleus)

6. Standing & Seated Calf Raises

⚙ Machine ★ Ankle Plantarflexion

Biomechanical Cue: Straight knees for standing raises (gastrocnemius); 90-degree bent knees for seated raises (soleus). Pause 2 seconds at the deep bottom stretch on every rep!

Target Head Gastrocnemius & Soleus
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 12-15
Rest 60s
Intensity RPE 9.0
Targeted Muscle: Biceps Long Head (Peak)

7. Incline Dumbbell Bicep Curl

⚙ Incline Bench ★ Peak Hypertrophy

Biomechanical Cue: Sit on a 45-degree incline bench with arms hanging fully behind torso. Curl dumbbells while supinating wrists outward at apex of movement.

Target Head Long Head (Outer Peak)
Sets & Reps 3 Sets × 10-12
Rest 60 - 90s
Intensity RPE 8.5
Targeted Muscle: Biceps Short Head (Width)

8. EZ-Bar Preacher Curl

⚙ Preacher Bench ★ Strict Isolation

Biomechanical Cue: Anchor upper arms firmly over preacher pad. Use slightly wider grip to emphasize inner short head. Lower under control without letting elbows hyperextend.

Target Head Short Head (Inner Width)
Sets & Reps 3 Sets × 10-12
Rest 60 - 90s
Intensity RPE 8.5
Targeted Muscle: Brachialis (Thickness)

9. Cross-Body Dumbbell Hammer Curls

⚙ Dumbbells ★ Neutral Grip

Biomechanical Cue: Keep palms facing inward. Curl dumbbell diagonally across chest toward opposite collarbone to bypass bicep supination and fire the deep brachialis.

Target Head Brachialis & Forearms
Sets & Reps 3 Sets × 10-12
Rest 60s
Intensity RPE 8.0
Targeted Muscle: Triceps Lateral Head (Horseshoe)

10. Cable Tricep Rope Pushdowns

⚙ Cable Machine ★ Lateral Sweep

Biomechanical Cue: Pin elbows tight against ribs. Push rope downward and spread rope tips apart at bottom lockout to intensely engage outer horseshoe head.

Target Head Lateral Head
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 10-12
Rest 60s
Intensity RPE 8.5
Targeted Muscle: Triceps Long Head (Inner Mass)

11. Overhead Cable Tricep Extension

⚙ High Cable & Rope ★ Overhead Stretch

Biomechanical Cue: Face away from high pulley. Keep elbows alongside ears. Allow cable to pull elbows into deep flexion behind head before extending into full lockout.

Target Head Long Head Mass
Sets & Reps 3 Sets × 10-12
Rest 75s
Intensity RPE 8.5
Targeted Muscle: Lateral Deltoid (Width)

12. Dumbbell & Cable Lateral Raises

⚙ Dumbbells / Cable ★ V-Taper Builder

Biomechanical Cue: Raise arms in scapular plane (30 degrees forward of direct lateral). Lead with elbows, stop at shoulder height, and control 3-second lowering.

Target Head Lateral (Side) Deltoid
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 12-15
Rest 60s
Intensity RPE 9.0
Targeted Muscle: Anterior Deltoid (Front)

13. Standing Overhead Barbell Press

⚙ Barbell ★ Vertical Push

Biomechanical Cue: Stand tall with core braced and glutes locked. Press bar straight up past chin, shifting head through window at top lockout directly over mid-foot.

Target Head Anterior Deltoid
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 6-8
Rest 120s
Intensity RPE 8.5
Targeted Muscle: Posterior Deltoid (Rear)

14. Cable Rope Face Pulls

⚙ Cable Machine ★ Postural Balance

Biomechanical Cue: Set pulley at eye level. Pull rope toward bridge of nose while externally rotating hands back so thumbs point behind you. Squeeze rear delts hard.

Target Head Posterior Deltoid
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 15-20
Rest 60s
Intensity RPE 8.0
Targeted Muscle: Latissimus Dorsi (Lats)

15. Wide-Grip Lat Pulldowns & Pull-ups

⚙ Cable / Bodyweight ★ V-Taper Wings

Biomechanical Cue: Depress scapulae first before pulling elbows down and in toward ribcage. Avoid excessive backward lean to keep mechanical load isolated on latissimus fibers.

Target Head Latissimus Dorsi
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 8-10
Rest 90s
Intensity RPE 8.5
Targeted Muscle: Trapezius & Rhomboids

16. Chest-Supported T-Bar Rows

⚙ Leverage Machine ★ Upper Back Density

Biomechanical Cue: Anchor chest firmly against support pad. Flare elbows out to 60 degrees. Retract shoulder blades fully together at peak contraction and hold for 1 second.

Target Head Traps & Rhomboids
Sets & Reps 4 Sets × 8-10
Rest 90s
Intensity RPE 8.5
